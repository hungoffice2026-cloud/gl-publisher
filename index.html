A video queue that posts itself, on time, every day.

GL Publisher takes videos a brand has already produced, holds them in a dated queue, and publishes two of them each morning to TikTok, YouTube and Facebook. It then writes back what happened to each one, so nothing is posted twice and nothing is quietly skipped.

Who it is for

Small food and beverage producers who film in batches. A kitchen shoots thirty clips in one afternoon, then has no one free to post them for the next six weeks. The videos sit on a drive and age out.

GL Publisher is the piece in between: a spreadsheet of what to post and when, and a scheduled job that does the posting. There is no feed to check and no app to open in the morning.

How it works

  1. Fill the queue

    Each row of a spreadsheet holds one video: the file, the publish date, and the caption written for each platform. Captions differ because the platforms differ.

  2. Connect the accounts

    You authorise GL Publisher once per platform. Authorisation is granted by you, in each platform's own consent screen, and can be withdrawn there at any time.

  3. Publishing runs on schedule

    At the set hour, the next two unpublished rows are read. Each video is fetched from storage and uploaded to each connected platform with its caption.

  4. Results are written back

    Every row records the outcome per platform and the link to the published post. A platform that fails is marked as failed with its error, and the other two still publish.

What GL Publisher does not do

  • It does not read, send or store private messages.
  • It does not follow, like, comment on, or view other accounts' content.
  • It does not generate, buy or simulate views, followers or engagement.
  • It does not edit, re-encode or alter video. Files are posted exactly as supplied.
  • It does not post anything that is not already in the queue the account owner wrote.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer account data to anyone.

Current status

GL Publisher is operated privately for the food brands run by its developer in Ho Chi Minh City. Posting to YouTube and Facebook Pages is in daily use. TikTok posting is in development against the Content Posting API and is limited to the developer's own account while under review.

If you have a question about the tool or about data held for an account you control, write to us through support.
