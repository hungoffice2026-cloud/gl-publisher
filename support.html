Deleting your data

Send the account or Page name and ask for removal. Stored tokens and identifiers are deleted and you get written confirmation. No reason is needed and nothing is charged. See the privacy policy for what is held.

Disconnecting an account

You do not need us for this. Withdraw access on the platform itself and publishing stops at once.

  • TikTok — Profile, then Settings and privacy, then Security and permissions, then Manage app permissions.
  • YouTube and Google — Google Account, then Security, then Your connections to third-party apps and services.
  • Facebook — Settings, then Business integrations.

A post did not go out

Check the queue row first. Each row records the result for each platform and the error the platform returned. The common causes are below.

Common reasons a scheduled post fails
What you seeUsual causeWhat to do
One platform failed, the other two postedThat platform's authorisation expired or its API returned an errorReconnect that platform, then set the row back to unpublished
Nothing posted at allThe machine running the schedule was offline at the publishing hourPosts resume at the next scheduled run; move the dates forward if order matters
Upload quota reachedThe platform's daily API limit was hitWait for the platform's quota to reset, then republish the row
Video rejected by the platformLength, aspect ratio, file size or content rulesRe-export the file within that platform's limits
Posted but not visible publiclyThe account is still restricted to private posting, or the channel is unverifiedVerify the account on the platform, then republish

Reporting a problem

Include the date of the run, the platform, and the error text from the queue row. That is normally enough to identify the cause without any further access.
