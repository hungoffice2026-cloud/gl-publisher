Privacy policy

Last updated 6 October 2026

GL Publisher is a scheduling tool that posts videos to social media accounts on behalf of the person who owns those accounts. This page describes exactly what it holds, why, and for how long.

Who is responsible

GL Publisher is built and operated by an independent developer based in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. Contact details are on the support page.

What is collected

  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, YouTube and Facebook when you authorise the tool. These let it upload on your behalf. They are not passwords and give no access to your login.
  • Account and Page identifiers — the account or Page ID the tokens belong to, so uploads go to the right destination.
  • Your display name or Page name, returned by the platform during authorisation, shown so you can confirm the right account is connected.
  • The content you queue — your video files, captions, hashtags and publish dates.
  • Publishing results — whether each post succeeded, the link to the published post, and any error returned by the platform.

What is not collected

  • Your password for any platform. Authorisation happens on the platform's own site; GL Publisher never sees it.
  • Private messages, direct messages or comments.
  • Your followers, your following list, or other users' content.
  • Audience analytics, viewer demographics or engagement data.
  • Payment details. The tool does not process payments.

How it is used

Everything collected is used for one purpose: uploading the videos you queued to the accounts you connected, at the time you chose, and recording the outcome. It is not used for advertising, profiling, training machine-learning models, or any form of analysis beyond showing you whether a post went out.

Where it is stored

Tokens are held in an encrypted credential store on a private machine controlled by the operator. Queues and publishing results are held in the account owner's own spreadsheet and file storage. Video files are read at publishing time and are not retained by the tool after upload.

Who it is shared with

No one. Data is not sold, rented, traded or shared with advertisers, data brokers or any third party. The only transfers that occur are to the social platforms themselves — TikTok, YouTube and Facebook — and only to carry out the upload you queued. Each of those platforms handles that data under its own privacy policy.

How long it is kept

  • Tokens are kept while the connection is active, and deleted when you revoke access or ask us to remove them.
  • Publishing results are kept in the account owner's spreadsheet for as long as the owner keeps it.
  • Temporary copies of video files made during upload are discarded once the upload finishes or fails.

Revoking access

You can disconnect GL Publisher at any time, directly on the platform, without contacting us. Access stops immediately.

  • TikTok — Profile, then Settings and privacy, then Security and permissions, then Manage app permissions.
  • YouTube and Google — Google Account, then Security, then Your connections to third-party apps and services.
  • Facebook — Settings, then Business integrations, or Page settings, then Advanced messaging and connected apps.

Deleting your data

Write to the address on the support page with the account or Page name. Stored tokens and identifiers for that account are deleted and you receive written confirmation. Requests are answered within 30 days and normally within a few days.

Your rights

You may ask what is held about you, ask for it to be corrected, ask for it to be deleted, or withdraw your authorisation. None of these requests carries a charge, and none requires a reason.

Children

GL Publisher is a tool for business accounts and is not directed at children. It is not knowingly made available to anyone under the minimum age set by the platform they are connecting.

Security

Credentials are stored encrypted at rest. Access to the machine running the tool is restricted to the operator. All communication with platform APIs uses HTTPS. No security measure is perfect; if a breach affects your data, you will be told.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it. Material changes affecting connected accounts are notified to the account owner directly.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to the address on the support page.
